# Rogue Class Guide

**Version:** 0.3.6

The Rogue is a Dexterity-based assassin and shadow master who excels at precision strikes and battlefield control. They specialize in sneak attacks with scaling damage, defensive evasion, and tactical mobility. Rogues reward smart positioning and exploiting advantages.

### ![](assets/expertise_icon.webp) Expertise *(L1)*
Passive. Years of training sharpen your reflexes, granting **+2 Dexterity**.

### ![](assets/cunning_instincts_icon.webp) Cunning Instincts *(L3)*
Passive. Your quick wits give you **+2 to initiative rolls**.

### ![](assets/uncanny_dodge_icon.webp) Uncanny Dodge *(L5)*
Passive. Your instinctive evasion **halves all physical damage** you receive (resistance to physical damage).

### ![](assets/evasion_icon.webp) Evasion *(L7)*
Passive. Your nimble form grants **advantage on Dexterity saving throws**.

### ![](assets/extra_attack_icon.webp) Extra Attack *(L11)*
Passive. Grants one additional attack per turn.

### ![](assets/slippery_mind_icon.webp) Slippery Mind *(L14)*
Passive. Your resilient psyche grants **advantage on Wisdom saving throws**.

### ![](assets/shadow_mastery_icon.webp) Shadow Mastery *(L17)*
Passive. You move through shadows with supernatural grace, gaining **+4 Dexterity** and **advantage on all attack rolls**.

### ![](assets/deaths_edge_icon.webp) Death's Edge *(L20)*
Passive. You are the apex of lethality. Every strike finds the vital spot, adding **4d6 physical damage** to all attacks.

Attacks are chosen on level up and added to your action list. Most require advantage on attacks or the Shadow Step condition to activate.

| ![](assets/hide_icon.webp) | Hide | 0 | Slip into concealment, becoming **Hidden** for up to 5 turns. Grants advantage on attack rolls until an enemy spots you (Intelligence check). |

## Tips

- **Dexterity is your primary stat.** It drives Expertise, Uncanny Dodge, Evasion, initiative, and all attack rolls. Stack it.
- **Sneak Attack (L1)** is your signature ability. It scales from **1d6** at L1 to **10d6** at L20, but requires advantage on attacks. Use Shadow Step or Shadow Mastery to enable it.
- **Shadow Step (L5)** is your setup tool — use it to gain advantage, then Sneak Attack the next turn for massive damage.
- **Uncanny Dodge (L5)** makes you surprisingly tanky. Physical resistance cuts incoming melee damage in half, and combined with Evasion advantage, you're hard to kill.
- **Shadow Mastery (L17)** is a game-changer — permanent advantage on all attacks means Sneak Attack becomes your default, not a special condition.
- **Death's Edge (L20)** adds 4d6 to every attack, stacking with Sneak Attack dice for truly devastating damage.
- **Death Mark (L7)** is powerful crowd control — mark the biggest threat and your whole party gains advantage against it for 2 turns.
- The Rogue rewards positioning and planning — set up advantages, use them strategically, and watch enemies fall to precision strikes.
