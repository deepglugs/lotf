# Succubus Class Guide

**Version:** 0.4.96

The Succubus is a Lust-aligned controller. Rather than trading blows, she takes
enemies out of the fight: stunning them, distracting them, leaving them unable
to act — and, when she needs to, disappearing from the battlefield entirely.
Her offence comes from the cold: a Succubus soaks her enemies, then freezes
them.

Her key stat is **Charisma**. Every save she forces uses the same difficulty:

> **Save DC = 8 + Charisma modifier + (level ÷ 4, rounded down)**

## Succubus Invisibility

![](assets/invisible_icon.webp)

The succubus trick is to be noticed. This is the opposite: pulling every thread
of her own presence in until nothing can find her.

- While **Invisible**, enemies cannot choose you as a target as long as someone
  visible is still standing. If you are the last one left, you can be targeted
  anyway.
- **It can be seen through.** Each enemy makes a **Wisdom check** against your
  save DC once per turn. On a success, that enemy can target you normally for
  the rest of the turn. Perceptive creatures are the danger here.
- The same rules apply when an *enemy* turns invisible: your characters roll
  Wisdom to spot it, and it is hidden from your target list if they fail.

**Pegasus's version.** Pegasus learns this ability from Igret in Chapter 3,
before she has any lust to spend. Her copy costs nothing but can only be used
**once per rest**. The Succubus class version costs 2 Lust and has no limit.

## Tips

- **Soak, then freeze.** Golden Shower (or Sleet Storm) leaves enemies Wet,
  and Wet doubles cold damage. Follow up with Glacial Strike or Blizzard.
- **Don't soak what you mean to burn.** Wet also gives resistance to heat, so a
  Wet target shrugs off half of your Heat damage-over-time.
- **Charisma is everything.** Every save you force — Seduce, Command,
  Enthralling Chant, Horny, Invisibility — runs off your Charisma DC.
- **Stack the lockdown.** Seduce and Command stun single targets; Enthralling
  Chant and Sleet Storm can each cost a whole group their turns. Between them, a
  Succubus can keep most of a fight from acting.
- **Invisibility is an escape, not an opener.** Save it for when you are hurt or
  being focused. Against enemies with high Wisdom it is much less reliable.
- **Masturbate is a battery.** Early on, when your lust pool is small, a turn
  spent recovering energy can pay for Seduce and Golden Shower later.
