# HolyKnight Class Guide

**Version:** 0.6

The HolyKnight is the **Heavenfire affinity class**, granted free when Chrys gains the Heavenfire affinity. It's a Charisma-driven martial class that passes judgement on the battlefield. It marks foes so the whole party hits them harder, sets them burning with holy fire, turns undead and demons, and takes hits meant for its allies. The Paladin owns smites and auras; the HolyKnight's job is judgement.

Abilities spend **Heavenfire energy** (pool ≈ 3 × HolyKnight level + modifier).

### ![](assets/slap_icon.webp) Divine Bitch Slap *(L1 — 4 Heavenfire)*
**2d8 radiant** plus 1d4 for every four character levels (up to 5d4 more), plus your Strength modifier as physical damage.

### ![](assets/smite_icon.webp) Divine Smite *(L2 — 8 Heavenfire)*
Normal weapon damage plus **2d8 radiant**, 1d8 more for every four levels (up to 5d8 more), and another **1d8 against undead and demons**. The radiant dice double on a critical hit.

### ![](assets/heal_icon.webp) Heal *(L2 — 6 Heavenfire)*
Heals an ally for 1d4 per four levels (at least 1d4) plus the **better of your Wisdom and Charisma** modifiers.

### ![](assets/zealous_conviction_icon.webp) Zealous Conviction *(L3, passive)*
Add your **Charisma modifier** to the radiant damage of Divine Bitch Slap, Divine Smite and Heavenfire Blade.

### ![](assets/extra_attack_icon.webp) Extra Attack *(L5, passive)*
One extra action per turn.

### ![](assets/unyielding_faith_icon.webp) Unyielding Faith *(L7, passive)*
**Advantage on Wisdom and Constitution saving throws.** That makes you hard to stun, charm or frighten.

Passives missed by an older save (a HolyKnight already past level 3 or 7 when they were added) are granted at the next HolyKnight level-up.

## Judgement Abilities

Chosen as the level-up option, one per level from those unlocked. Save DCs are **8 + proficiency + Charisma modifier**.

| Unlocks | Icon | Ability | Cost | Effect |
|---------|------|---------|------|--------|
| L1 | ![](assets/call_judgement_icon.webp) | Call Judgement | 3 | Target is **Judged** for 3 turns: every weapon hit on it adds 1d6 radiant (2d6 from L11) |
| L2 | ![](assets/heavenfire_blade_icon.webp) | Heavenfire Blade | 4 | 1d8 + Cha radiant, then burns 1d6 a turn for 3 turns |
| L3 | ![](assets/turn_the_profane_icon.webp) | Turn the Profane | 5 | Undead and demons: Wisdom save or **Turned** for 2 turns. Once per battle |
| L5 | ![](assets/guardians_oath_icon.webp) | Guardian's Oath | 3 | Half the damage an ally takes goes to you, for 3 turns |
| L14 | ![](assets/black_hole_icon.webp) | Black Hole | — | Tri-affinity capstone; needs all three affinities |

### ![](assets/call_judgement_icon.webp) Call Judgement *(L1)*
Call heaven's judgement on one enemy. It never misses. For 3 turns the target is **Judged**, and **every weapon hit against it, from anyone in the party**, deals an extra **1d6 radiant** (**2d6** from HolyKnight level 11). Cast it on whatever the party is focusing.

### ![](assets/heavenfire_blade_icon.webp) Heavenfire Blade *(L2)*
Set your blade alight and strike (a normal attack roll). On a hit it deals **1d8 + Charisma radiant**, with one more d8 at HolyKnight levels 5, 11 and 17. Dice double on a crit. The target then **burns for 1d6 radiant each turn for 3 turns**, or **2d6 against undead and demons**.

### ![](assets/turn_the_profane_icon.webp) Turn the Profane *(L3)*
Blaze with holy light. Every **undead and demon** enemy makes a Wisdom save or is **Turned** for 2 turns: it cowers and can't act, with a Wisdom save at the end of each turn to break free. Other enemies are unaffected, and the button greys out when there are no undead or demons. **Once per battle.**

### ![](assets/guardians_oath_icon.webp) Guardian's Oath *(L5)*
Swear to shield an ally (not yourself) for 3 turns. **Half of all damage they take goes to you instead**, as long as you're standing. If you fall, the oath stops protecting them.

## Tips

- **Judge, then swing.** Call Judgement on turn one, and every party member's weapon hits on that target get the bonus. It pays off more the more martial characters you have.
- **Heavenfire Blade is your damage-over-time attack.** Put it on a tough target, then go back to Smite or weapon attacks while it burns. It hits undead and demons twice as hard.
- **Turn the Profane** can shut down a whole undead or demon pack for a couple of turns. The Descent to Hell's demon floors and Ch3's barrows are where it shines.
- **Guardian's Oath** suits a HolyKnight with high HP and Constitution. Use it on a caster who's taking focus fire.
- **Charisma does everything:** save DCs, Heavenfire Blade, Zealous Conviction and Heal.
